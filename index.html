Portable AC EV charger tester designed for Type 2 charging piles. It supports interoperability testing, on-site metering verification, stable anti-interference testing, searchable test data, and safe portable operation in complex field environments.

  • Reference standards: IEC 62196-2:2022, IEC 61851-1:2017, IEC 61851-21, IEC 62196-1:2022.
  • Optional multi-channel high-precision high-speed waveform acquisition for charging voltage, current, and pilot signals.
  • Real-time visualization of voltage, current, power, state, and charging sequence diagrams.
  • Message export and parsing for charger status analysis and fault diagnosis.

STELR-DC1000V-120kW Regenerative DC Electronic Load

The STELR regenerative DC electronic load uses DCDC control and PWM inverter technology with dual DSP control. It delivers high-performance full-power energy feedback to the grid, improving energy efficiency compared with conventional DC loads.

  • Supports automatic CC/CV modes and bidirectional high-voltage battery simulation.
  • Supports constant-voltage non-full-current testing, constant-current regulated full-current testing, DC overvoltage simulation, and long-term aging tests.
  • Emergency stop and external emergency stop functions protect connected equipment during abnormal operation.
  • Output self-discharge function quickly reduces terminal voltage to a safe level after shutdown.
