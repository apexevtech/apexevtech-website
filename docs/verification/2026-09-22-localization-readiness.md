# Localization readiness review — 2026-09-22

## Current evidence

The latest available 28-day Search Console country export shows 68 impressions from the United States, 34 from Germany and 11 from the United Kingdom, with one recorded click in total. Other markets have five or fewer impressions each. This is not enough demand to select and maintain a full second technical language.

## Decision

Keep English as the single canonical site language for now. Do not publish machine-translated or partially translated indexable pages; they would duplicate the site structure without reliable technical review or proven search demand.

## Launch criteria for a second language

Add a locale only when:

- one non-English market produces sustained impressions, qualified inquiries or sales priority;
- product specifications, standards terminology, forms and privacy text can be reviewed by a fluent technical reviewer;
- the complete core journey can be translated: homepage, product list, relevant product pages, contact and privacy;
- each localized URL can receive a self-canonical and reciprocal `hreflang`, including `x-default` on the English version.

German is the first market to reassess if its exposure continues to rise. The next review should use at least 8–12 weeks of Search Console and inquiry-country data.
