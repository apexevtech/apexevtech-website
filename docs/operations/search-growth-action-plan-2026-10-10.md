## CTR decision rules

Prioritize a snippet test only when a page has at least 30 impressions in the review period and an average position of 15 or better. A low CTR at position 20 or lower is primarily a ranking problem, because the result is rarely visible on the first page.

For an eligible page, change one title or description direction at a time, record the deployment date and wait for recrawl. Compare equivalent complete periods. Keep the query phrase, page purpose and visible opening answer aligned because Google may generate the snippet from page content rather than the meta description.
