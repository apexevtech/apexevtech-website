## Extended description

Apex Power Systems (Nanjing) Co., Ltd. provides EV charger and EVSE test equipment for manufacturers, laboratories, inspection teams, charging-station operators and field-service organizations. APEX systems support AC and DC charger validation across development, end-of-line testing, laboratory verification, commissioning and maintenance workflows. Available configurations address GB/T, CCS, IEC, CHAdeMO, NACS and related charging interfaces, with options for communication analysis, vehicle or BMS simulation, electrical measurement, waveform capture, fault simulation, metering verification and external-load integration. Project scope, standards editions, ratings and optional modules are confirmed before quotation.
