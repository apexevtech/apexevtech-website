## Public Data Boundary

`data/engineering-cases.ts` contains original English summaries of the described
technical scopes, not translated full articles. The stable `case-1` to `case-18`
identifiers preserve the internal detail-page mapping. Meaningful slugs and
titles describe technical topics rather than naming the original organizations.

The public data contains no external source links, customer logos, delivery
dates, product-model lists, quantitative success claims or claims that these
were APEX deliveries. The page must describe them as partner-published
references rather than verified APEX delivery records.

Case 5 remains a title-level reference without readable project detail. Case 8
does not acquire an invented measurement workflow. Historical standards in case
7 are not rewritten as current conformity coverage. Unsupported AI, efficiency,
automatic adjustment and throughput claims are not adopted as verified results.

The image mappings are limited to the existing authorized local images for
cases 1, 11 and 14, plus the matching original images for cases 2 and 5 reviewed
on 2026-10-21. See
`saiter-authorized-image-register-2026-10-20.md` for the original authorization
record and image-selection boundary. Do not fill missing case images using an
unrelated case or generic site photograph.
